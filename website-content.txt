Alef Delta – HR & Workforce Management Platform
Alef Delta is a modern HR and workforce management platform that lets employees focus on their talent and performance, while companies stop worrying about day‑to‑day HR hassles such as hiring, attendance, payroll, and compliance.
What Alef Delta Does
Alef Delta connects companies that need reliable staff with employees who want stable, well‑managed jobs.
The platform handles the full employee lifecycle:
From recruitment to onboarding
From daily attendance to GPS‑backed timesheets
From shift planning to payroll with penalties, bonuses, and tax/VAT
From incident reporting to client billing and management
Companies no longer need to follow up each salary manually or chase attendance sheets; Alef Delta centralizes everything into a single, professional system.
Who Alef Delta Is For
For Employees
Job seekers and field staff who want:
Transparent shifts and schedules
Clear salary and payment history
Simple, mobile‑first tools to clock in and out
A trusted HR partner that manages contracts and compliance with client companies
For Companies
Businesses that need employees without HR headaches, including:
Companies that hire security guards, cleaners, promoters, drivers, field sales, and other field staff
Organizations that work with multiple sites, branches, and clients
Managers who want strong control over attendance, performance, and payroll without doing every calculation by hand
Companies can rely on Alef Delta to provide, manage, and monitor employees, so they do not need to worry about recruitment, testing, daily attendance, or individual salary processing.
Core Components of the System
Staff Portal (Admin Panel)
A web‑based admin portal for office and management users (Owner, GM, HR, Finance, Operations, Marketing, Procurement). It provides:
Role‑based access control so each department only sees what they need
Complete visibility of jobs, employees, clients, sites, attendance, payroll, billing, and reports
Central place to manage user accounts and roles
Member Portal (Employee Portal)
A mobile‑friendly member portal for field staff that allows:
Viewing today’s shift, future roster, and assigned client site
Clock in / clock out with GPS and location context
Viewing salary history and payslips
Reporting incidents or issues from the field
Working even with poor internet, using offline‑aware features that sync later
Telegram Mini App for Employees
Alef Delta also provides a Telegram Mini App for employees:
Employees can use Telegram as their daily HR interface
View shift schedule, attendance status, and salary details from their phone
Submit clock in/out (where allowed) with location, notes, or simple confirmations
Receive notifications about upcoming shifts, approvals, or HR updates
This makes adoption very easy, because employees do not have to install a separate app.
Key Features – System Capabilities
1. Advanced Attendance & Time Tracking
GPS‑based attendance:
Capture location when employees clock in and out
Verify presence at the correct client site
Multiple attendance modes:
Automatic / GPS attendance
Manual attendance (for corrections or locations with no GPS)
Mixed attendance model combining device logs, supervisor approval, and manual adjustments
Shift & roster integration:
Attendance is validated against planned shifts and working days
Late arrivals, early leaves, absences, and overtime can be tracked accurately
Permission and leave management:
Record permissions, approved absences, and exceptions
Configurable rules to mark with‑permission / without‑permission absences
2. Smart Roster & Shift Management
Create and manage shift schedules for each employee, team, and client site
Support different working-day patterns and rotations
Operational users can:
Assign staff to clients and sites
See who is on duty today, tomorrow, and in coming weeks
Avoid double‑booking or understaffed sites
3. Advanced Payroll Engine
Alef Delta includes an advanced payroll system that calculates salaries based on real operational data:
Attendance‑based payroll:
Automatically considers presence, absence, late, overtime, and shift type
Supports penalties for unapproved absence or lateness
Supports bonuses for performance, extra shifts, or incentives
Configurable penalties and overrides:
HR can define standard penalties and bonuses per job or employee
Operations can set site‑specific rules where needed
Tax & VAT‑aware calculations:
Payroll integrates with invoice and billing structure for clients
Supports tax/VAT elements so finance can stay compliant
Salary history and transparency:
Employees can see salary breakdowns and history in the member portal
Finance can extract detailed reports for accounting and audits
4. Recruitment & HR Management
Vacancies and job applications:
Create and publish job vacancies
Track applications and status
Screening and onboarding:
Manage job application screenings and employee screenings
Collect and track employee documents (ID, contracts, certificates)
Employee profiles:
Maintain full employee records (contact details, positions, departments, history)
Auto‑generate secure user accounts and default passwords for new employees
5. Client & Site Management
Register client organizations and their sites/locations
Link employees to specific clients and sites
Manage service levels, staffing requirements, and incidents per site
Store contact information and preferences for each client
6. CRM & Business Development (Leads & Bids)
Track CRM leads from potential client companies
Manage bids / proposals and their status
Coordinate marketing and sales activities inside the same platform
7. Billing & Invoicing
Generate client invoices based on:
Staff assignments
Attendance and shift data
Agreed billing rates and VAT
Attach supporting documents (e.g. reports, summaries)
Monitor payment status and integrate with finance workflows
8. Incident & Operational Reporting
Field staff and supervisors can report incidents (security, safety, service issues)
Operations team can:
Log, categorize, and track incidents
Record who reported them and from which site
Management can review incident trends and respond quickly
9. Assets & Procurement (As Implemented)
Register and manage assets related to staff and operations such as:
Uniforms, fuel cards, vehicles, equipment, and office items
Link assets to clients, sites, or employees
Support procurement workflows and cost tracking
10. Dashboards & Reporting
Role‑based dashboards for Owner, GM, HR, Finance, Operations, Marketing, and Procurement
Overview of:
Attendance status and trends
Payroll and cost summaries
Client performance and incidents
Recruitment pipeline and CRM activity
Exportable reports for management, finance, and auditors
